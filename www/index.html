Chess 1v1 Rules

Face-to-Face Offline Play

Place the device flat between both players. In Opposite Sides mode, the top player's pieces and status bar rotate 180° so both players view their own pieces upright from opposite ends. Switch to Same Side when sitting side by side.

Check & Checkmate

When a King is attacked, its square glows crimson with a ! marker and the player's banner shows In Check. Only legal moves that resolve check are allowed. If no legal move exists while in check, the square marks # and the game ends in Checkmate.

All Official Chess Rules

  • Castling: Supported kingside and queenside when neither King nor Rook has moved, squares between are clear, and the King does not start, pass through, or land in check.
  • En Passant: Capture an enemy pawn immediately after its two-square opening advance, including horizontal pin verification.
  • Pawn Promotion: Reaching the far rank opens an oriented picker to promote to Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.
  • Draws: Enforces Stalemate, Insufficient Material, Threefold & Fivefold Repetition, and the 50/75-Move rules.
